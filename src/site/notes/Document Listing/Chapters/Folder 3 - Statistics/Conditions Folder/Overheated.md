An overheated unit is incapable of using energy-based actions and abilities until the condition is lost.
- At the end of its turn, the unit makes a mark 5 test with d4. Overheat persists for 3 turns, or until the unit hits its mark. On a success, recover 1 energy point, making the generator useable again. At extreme, recover all energy.
