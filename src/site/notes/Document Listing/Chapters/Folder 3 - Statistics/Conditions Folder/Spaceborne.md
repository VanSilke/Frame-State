A spaceborne unit's state is fluid based on its corrective actions.
- A spaceborne unit can normally move 1 hex per turn, unless it flies.

At the beginning of its turn, a spaceborne unit has two to three primary options:
- Spend 1 energy and count as grounded;
- Don't spend energy and count as airborne for statistical adjustments;
- Don't spend energy and count as airborne, and begin drifting in space.

While drifting in space, the unit moves 1 hex per turn in a random direction, or at the same speed as it was before this point. For example, if the unit flew 40 meters during its turn and then started drifting, it would drift 40 meters per round until somehow stopped. 
- When staggered, a unit continues to drift until it recovers.
- Space drifting occurs when all forced movement does - in an environmental direction.
