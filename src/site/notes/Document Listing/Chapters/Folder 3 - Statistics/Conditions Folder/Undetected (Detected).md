The location of an undetected unit is unknown to the relative observer. It is considered out of range for all weapons. This is most often because the unit is behind a non-transparent piece of terrain that obstructs the observer's field of view.

It is enough for the unit to get in the observer's line of sight to lose the undetected status. Then, it becomes detected, which is a default status for a unit within line of sight.
