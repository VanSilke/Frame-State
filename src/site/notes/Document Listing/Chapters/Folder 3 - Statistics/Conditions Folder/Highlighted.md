A highlighted unit has been marked by another unit, known as the highlighter. The highlighter always knows where the highlighted unit is down to a hex, even when it is cloaked or otherwise out of sight, and can communicate this location with allies.

When a highlighted unit is surveyed, its exact location is revealed, and it loses all relevant benefits, such as from cloaking.
