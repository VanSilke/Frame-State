A staggered unit is in a dire situation.

When a unit's Stability reaches 0 at any point, it becomes staggered. This indicates that the unit cannot act until it successfully stabilises.

When staggered, the unit can perform no other actions than to attempt to stabilise, and does not remove its staggered condition until it regains all stability.

For as long as the unit is not staggered, it is considered Stable.
