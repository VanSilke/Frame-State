Spend 1 step to make an attempt to hide with a Smarts test. You may choose a mark between 3 and 7. 
- On a success, you become cloaked until you move or use a weapon. 
- On an extreme, you don't lose cloaking from performing other actions. 
- The cloaking lasts for a maximum of up to the end of your next turn.
- The mark you chose is also the mark required for tests made to highlight you.
