Spend 1 step to find points of interest with a Sharpness test. You don't know the mark, and the Handler declares the outcome. For example, if a unit was cloaked with mark 5 and your score was 4, you wouldn't know anything other than that the survey attempt was a failure.
- On a success, you highlight all points of interest within your detection range until the end of your next turn.
- On an extreme (against cloaking effects) you also immediately ignore the now-highlighted point's cloaked status until the end of your turn. Normally, you would have to survey again to do so.
