Energy damage is less common, but scales well off the generator.
- Inflicts bonus damage based on the weapon's energy cost per use, at the rate of 1 bonus damage for 1 energy point.
- The unit can voluntarily increase energy cost up to its generator's maximum.
- This bonus damage, if mitigated, does not convert to impact.
