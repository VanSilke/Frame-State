**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 1 energy
**Trigger:**  You use a weapon.

You coat the payload with pulse energy. If the weapon hits, it deals an additional d8 points of impact. If the hit was against a pulse construct, such as that created by pulse armour, the pulse construct receives additional d8 points of damage.
