**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 1 energy
**Trigger:** You end your turn with at least 1 step remaining.

You choose one of your weapons to enter a ready state, and designate an area with a 30-meter radius. If you spot an enemy moving through, into, or out of the designated area, and the weapon is in range, you may automatically attack the enemy.
- If weapon use requires steps, you must end the turn with at least this many steps remaining.
- If a weapon requires other resources, they're paid for when the weapon is used, not readied.
