**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 1 step; 1 energy
**Trigger** You use a weapon.

You may spend additional time and energy using the weapon to ignore recoil. The weapon used in this way generates no recoil, and if you were already at 0 recoil, you ignore its penalties.
