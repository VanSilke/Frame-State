**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** None
**Trigger:**  You would be destroyed.

Your frame is rigged to explode when you would take critical damage. At the beginning of your next turn, you create an area of d10 blast damage, with a radius of 50 metres.
- If you have a pulse generator, the explosion's damage type switches to energy and deals an additional d8 damage to pulse constructs. You may sacrifice all your remaining energy to boost the damage of the explosion, as normal for energy weapons.
- If you have a wave generator, the explosion's damage type switches to wave, dealing direct hit damage to staggered units.
- If you have a singularity generator, the explosion's damage type switches to singularity, such that it cannot be mitigated.

Unless you have the means of somehow surviving the blast, you die along with the wreck, which becomes irrecoverable.

Finally, you can activate the module on your own by pressing the ominous red button, in which case the destruction occurs at the beginning of your next turn but you can still act to eject or do something else.
