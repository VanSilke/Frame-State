**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 1 step; 1 energy

You generate a cloud of detection-interfering smoke and chaff in yours and adjacent hexes. You cannot be highlighted for as long as you remain in the cloud. If you were highlighted, you clear that state. Additionally, each hex of the cloud breaks up line of sight.

The cloud remains until the beginning of your next turn, and then disperses. A shockwave from blasts clears the cloud immediately.
