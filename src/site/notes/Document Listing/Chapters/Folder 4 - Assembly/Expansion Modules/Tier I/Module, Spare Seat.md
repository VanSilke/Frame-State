**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 2 steps (yours and passenger's each)

Your frame features enough internal space to hold a passenger. While inside your frame, the passenger is safe from external harm, but shares your fate in case the frame gets destroyed.

In order to board your frame, you must get it down and open a hatch with a step. The passenger (who must be in the same hex) must spend two steps climbing into position. You must then spend one step to close the hatch and return to operational state.

If the passenger can't climb in during your turn (for example if they had no leftover actions), you must keep yourself open until the passenger's next turn. During that time, you count as staggered.
