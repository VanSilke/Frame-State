**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** 1 step

You have a spare Tier I weapon (statted separately) which can be swapped for one of your other weapons, determined during assembly.

This weapon is normally inaccessible, until you use a step to swap to it. The swapped-from weapon then becomes inaccessible to you, until you swap back to it.
