**Installation:** TI
**Use Cost:** None
**Trigger:** The frame is destroyed.

Your wreck has an internal life support system and energy generator, enough to sustain a small emergency pod. Instead of ejecting, you are automatically moved to the black box when the frame is destroyed.

The black box remains in the frame's hex and has a total of 5 integrity. So long as the black box is not destroyed, you remain impervious to external harm and environmental hazards.

The black box comes with a voice recorder to memorialize your last moments.
