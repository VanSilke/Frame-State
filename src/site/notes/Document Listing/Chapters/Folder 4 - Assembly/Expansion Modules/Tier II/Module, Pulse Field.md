**Installation:** TII
**Cost:** 1 step; 1 energy

You generate a pulse construct of simple shape in a designated hex. It functions as a destructible, transparent, non-traversable prop with 10 integrity and no defences. The construct remains in place for 3 rounds or until destroyed, then it disperses.
