**Installation:** TII
**Cost:** 1 step; all energy

Your frame overheats, and becomes coated with a layer of pulse construct with 20 integrity points. Any hits you would take are taken by the coating instead. In some cases, this avoids certain status effects.
- Pulse armour takes all damage you would take, and negates all incoming impact. It cannot mitigate or negate any damage on itself.
- At the beginning of your turn, pulse armour loses 4 integrity automatically. Once it loses all integrity in this way, it disperses harmlessly. You can disperse pulse armour manually at your turn, with no cost.
- If the pulse armour would be destroyed by incoming damage, you become staggered.
