**Installation:** TIII
**Cost:** 1 step; 1 energy

You may designate yourself or another unit adjacent to your hex. The designated unit immediately regains 5 integrity points with no caveats.

Each point of integrity restored counts as integrity lost for the purposes of the mission costs tally.
