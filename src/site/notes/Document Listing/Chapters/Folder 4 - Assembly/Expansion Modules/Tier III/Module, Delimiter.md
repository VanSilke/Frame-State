**Installation:** TIII
**Cost:** 1 step; all energy

On use, the module overrides safety protocols, forcing the frame into overdrive. You overheat. You retain the ability to use energy abilities and weapons, spending integrity instead of energy. In addition, all weapons deal an additional 4 points of damage. Your frame takes d4 inevitable damage at the end of each turn until you exit overdrive.

Exiting overdrive can  be done at the beginning of your turn, and costs no resources, but does not rid of the overheat status.
