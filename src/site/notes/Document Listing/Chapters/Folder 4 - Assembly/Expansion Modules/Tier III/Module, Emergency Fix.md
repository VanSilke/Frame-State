**Installation:** TIII
**Cost:** 1 step; all energy

On use, choose a value up to 10. Reduce your maximum integrity by this amount. You regain this many integrity points, up to the new lowered maximum. Then, you overheat.

Emergency fix can be performed more than once, each time decreasing maximum integrity. Maximum integrity lost in this way does not return until you receive proper repairs in assembly.
