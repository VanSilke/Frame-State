**Installation:** Variable Tier
**Cost:** 1 energy
**Trigger:** You move or fly.

You modify your speed thanks to additionally-installed boosters.

| Tiers | Outcome                  |
| :---: | ------------------------ |
|   I   | You add 4 to your speed. |
|  II   | You add 6 to your speed. |
|  III  | You add 8 to your speed. |
