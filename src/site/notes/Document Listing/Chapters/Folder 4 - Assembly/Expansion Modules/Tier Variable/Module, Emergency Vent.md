**Installation:** Variable Tier
**Cost:** none
**Trigger:** You overheat.

You open up your vents, expediating the generator restart. Your overheat tests take a bonus value, but the same value is reduced from your armour. The vents remain open until you recover from overheating.

| Tiers | Outcome                                                |
| :---: | ------------------------------------------------------ |
|   I   | You add 1 to overheating tests and reduce armour by 1. |
|  II   | You add 2 to overheating tests and reduce armour by 2. |
|  III  | You add 3 to overheating tests and reduce armour by 3. |
