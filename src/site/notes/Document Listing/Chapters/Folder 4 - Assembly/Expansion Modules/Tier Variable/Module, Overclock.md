**Installation:** Variable Tier
**Cost:** 1 energy
**Trigger:** You make an action that has an associated test, and costs 1 step or more.

As part of the action, you overclock your systems and add a die to the test. The exceptions are tests with no mark, such as damage or stabilisation rolls. The additional die is rolled alongside the normal one and may replace it if the natural result is higher.

| Tiers | Outcome                   |
| :---: | ------------------------- |
|   I   | You add d8 to your test.  |
|  II   | You add d10 to your test. |
|  III  | You add d12 to your test. |
