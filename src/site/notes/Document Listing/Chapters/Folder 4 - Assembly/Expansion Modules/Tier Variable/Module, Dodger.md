**Installation:** Variable Tier
**Cost:** 1 energy
**Trigger:** You dodge, move or fly.

You make use of quick-burst engines to move an additional distance, or if you're dodging, you move the distance and receive a bonus to dodge. If you cannot move, you cannot use this ability for dodging or moving. Otherwise, you can use this as many times as you like, even for the same action.

| Tiers | Outcome                              |
| :---: | ------------------------------------ |
|   I   | You add 1 to dodge and move 1 hex.   |
|  II   | You add 2 to dodge and move 2 hexes. |
|  III  | You add 3 to dodge and move 3 hexes. |
